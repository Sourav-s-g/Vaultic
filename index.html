Vaultic: Privacy Policies

Last Updated 18 July

Vaultic ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when you use the Vaultic mobile application (the "App").

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at contact.vaultic@gmail.com.

①Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address — used to create and access your account with your password. Password credentials are managed by our authentication provider, Supabase; Vaultic does not store them directly.

We do not currently collect your name, phone number, date of birth, or any other personal identifiers.

Financial Information You Enter

Vaultic is a personal finance tracking app. To provide its core functionality, we store the data you voluntarily enter, including:

  • Transactions and transaction categories
  • Budgets
  • "Owes/Owns" (OWO) entries
  • Backup snapshots of the above, for recovery purposes

This data reflects entries you make yourself. Vaultic does not connect to your bank account, card, or any financial institution, and we do not collect account numbers, card numbers, or credentials for any third-party financial service.

Locally Stored Data

Some of the data above is cached directly on your device for offline access and performance, in addition to being stored in the cloud.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts or address book data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device analytics or usage tracking data
  • Any data for advertising or cross-app tracking purposes

②How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Authenticate you with your email address and password, and maintain your session
  • Store, sync, and display the financial data you enter
  • Provide backup and recovery of your data across devices
  • Respond to support requests you send us

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to operating the App.

③How Your Data Is Stored

Vaultic uses Supabase as its backend infrastructure provider for authentication and cloud data storage. Supabase manages account authentication and stores your account and financial data on our behalf. Data in transit between the App and Supabase is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

We do not operate our own servers separate from this infrastructure, and we do not maintain a copy of your data outside of Supabase and your local device cache.

④Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or financial data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Your data is only accessible to:

  • You, through the App
  • Our infrastructure provider (Supabase), acting strictly as a data processor to operate the App
  • Us, if required to provide support or comply with a legal obligation

⑤Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may:

  • Clear all locally stored data at any time using the "Clear Local Data" option in Settings.
  • Request full deletion of your account and all associated cloud-stored data by emailing contact.vaultic@gmail.com. We will process deletion requests within a reasonable time and confirm once complete.

⑥Security

We rely on industry-standard practices to protect your data, including encrypted data transmission and password authentication managed by Supabase. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

⑦Children's Privacy

Vaultic is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required by your local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

⑧Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent to processing, where applicable

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact.vaultic@gmail.com.

⑨Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
